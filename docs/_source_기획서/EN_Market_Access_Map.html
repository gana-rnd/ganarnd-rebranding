This sets out what is registered where, what is in progress, and what cannot yet be offered. The market and distribution proposition needs it most, since the first question any distributor asks is whether they are covered in their own market.

Four separate chemistries under a single manufacturer — hyaluronic acid, PLLA, PCL and calcium. That range appears when a company formulates and fills across chemical families. It does not appear when a company buys one ingredient and resells it.

Description note. The registration holders are local representatives and GANA R&D appears in the manufacturer field. The accurate phrase is “named as manufacturer”, not “registered in our name”. For a manufacturing proposition this is the stronger claim in any case.

A note on the identifiers. “РЗН” is Cyrillic, not Latin — it is the abbreviation for Roszdravnadzor and reads R-Z-N. The searchable element is the numeric portion, for example 2024/22431, at roszdravnadzor.gov.ru. The same applies to the Korean licence numbers below: the Hangul prefix forms part of the official identifier.

7. How these should be described

A short set of conventions, so that anything said to a partner survives the check that follows it.

AvoidUse instead
“Registered in our name” (Russia)“Named as manufacturer” — the registration holders are local representatives
“FDA approved” · “FDA registered”Nothing — no US device registration exists
“Russia FDA” · “KFDA”Roszdravnadzor · MFDS — the first does not exist, the second was renamed in 2013
CE mark or ISO badgesNothing, until renewed and located
The UK Class III device brand name“Manufacturer registered with MHRA, MAN_ID 32678” — the device brand belongs to the market-authorisation holder and cannot be named until the manufacturing contract has been reviewed
Claims of in-house raw material synthesis“Combinations” and “formulation across chemical families” — the registrations evidence manufacturing scope, not ingredient synthesis
