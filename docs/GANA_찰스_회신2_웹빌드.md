## EN — 발송본

Charles, no formal review needed. Three fixes, then a build order.

**1. KoraVie DP+ belongs on the aesthetics page.**

I checked the register. DP+ (제허25-518) is a class 4 tissue-repair material, which is the same licence category as every one of our fillers. Shield (제인25-4996) is a class 2 wound dressing, a different category. In Korean regulation "tissue repair" is the fillers' category name, so with that word in the page title the two pages overlap.

Simplest fix: call the page **Wound Care**, and sort by buyer rather than material.
- Clinics buy it → Aesthetics: fillers, DP+ included
- Hospitals buy it → Wound Care: Shield, GANA PATCH, the foam dressing

KoraVie still appears on both pages.

**2. Device distributors need a home on the GANA R&D site.**

The Russian and Brazilian registrations are devices, not cosmetics. By your own attribution rule, the distribution offer can't live only on the cosmetics site. One partnerships block next to the market access map covers it: the map answers "am I covered", the block answers "what's the deal".

**3. One official capacity number before the AAV site goes up.**

Our Thermo Fisher release states 5,000 L per batch for Phase 1, and anyone doing diligence will read the site against it. My suggestion: Phase 1 numbers on the manufacturing page, and 200,000 L on the sovereign page as the full build-out target. The call is Dr. Park's. I'll put the question to him.

**Build order:**

1. This week: clean up the current sites. Zero cost, no decisions needed. Real registration numbers instead of "Russia FDA", expired badges down, current GMP certificate up, SSL fixed, domain email.
2. GANA R&D first. The content already exists in the map and the registration list, and it's where distributor revenue lands. English first.
3. Gateway, once the group name is settled.
4. AAV site, once the number is confirmed. Your proof lines already work as written.
5. Cosmetics last, refresh only. Its design system becomes the base for the other three.

If that works, I'll start on sitemaps.

## KR — 대조용 (발송하지 않음)

**1. KoraVie DP+는 미용 페이지 소속입니다.**

가장 간단한 해결: 페이지명을 **Wound Care**로 하고, 재료가 아니라 구매자로 나눕니다.
- 클리닉이 사는 것 → Aesthetics: 필러, DP+ 포함

KoraVie는 여전히 두 페이지 모두에 올라갑니다.

**2. 기기 유통사가 도착할 자리가 GANA R&D 사이트에 필요합니다.**

러시아·브라질 등재는 화장품이 아니라 의료기기입니다. 찰스님이 세우신 귀속 원칙대로면 유통 제안이 화장품 사이트에만 있을 수 없습니다. 시장접근 지도 옆에 파트너십 블록 하나면 됩니다. 지도가 "우리 지역 되나요"에 답하고, 블록이 "조건이 뭔가요"에 답합니다.

저희 Thermo Fisher 릴리스가 Phase 1을 배치당 5,000L로 명시하고 있고, 실사는 사이트를 그 릴리스와 대조해 읽습니다. 제안: Phase 1 수치는 제조 페이지에, 200,000L는 소버린 페이지에 완전 구축 목표로. 결정은 박사님 몫입니다. 질문은 제가 올리겠습니다.
